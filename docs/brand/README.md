# SailCast brand

A sloop's jib and mainsail rising out of two waves under the sun, on a deep-sea badge.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `sailcast-icon.svg` | The icon, a 512 × 512 badge. |
| `sailcast-logo.svg` | The logo for light backgrounds. |
| `sailcast-logo-dark.svg` | The logo for dark backgrounds. |

The PNGs Home Assistant uses are in [`custom_components/sailcast/brand/`](../../custom_components/sailcast/brand/): `icon.png` (256 × 256), `icon@2x.png` (512 × 512), and `logo.png`, `logo@2x.png`, `dark_logo.png` and `dark_logo@2x.png` (256 and 512 px tall). They have transparent backgrounds and are trimmed to the edges, as [Home Assistant's brand rules](https://github.com/home-assistant/brands#image-specification) ask. The icon works on light and dark, so there's no `dark_icon.png`.

**Colors:** the "Under the Wave" palette by [Paper Heart Design](https://paperheartdesign.com/blog/color-palette-peaceful-palettes): `#00022b`, `#010e54`, `#0855b1`, `#4fa5d8`, `#daeaf7`.

**Wordmark:** Outfit SemiBold, converted to outlines, so the SVGs need no font. Outfit is copyright 2021 The Outfit Project Authors ([github.com/Outfitio/Outfit-Fonts](https://github.com/Outfitio/Outfit-Fonts)) and licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org).
